A museum-style banknote viewing experience focused on visual appreciation, context, and historical memory.

Core Features

What You Can Do

  • Browse countries and denominations in a portrait-first gallery flow.
  • Use natural gestures: left/right to switch country, up/down to switch denomination.
  • Double-tap to explore obverse/reverse transitions.
  • Long-touch for motif information and tap to enter detailed note view.
  • Shake your iPhone to cycle through available gallery themes.
  • View structured metadata, source attribution, and contextual notes per banknote.
  • Use My Collection to add your own banknote images as a paid, local-only collection feature.
  • Review and edit collection details with local OCR assistance, text-based reference lookup, rotation, image swapping, and high-confidence auto-crop support.
Technology & Craft

Experience Design

  • Built with SwiftUI and SwiftData for responsive, native interaction.
  • Motion behavior is tuned to feel natural, with subtle drift and touch-responsive micro movement during viewing.
  • Currency switching uses staged transition timing, so flips, slides, and settle moments read clearly.
  • Visual direction emphasizes artistic presentation of engraving detail, motifs, and historical aesthetics.
  • Audio and tactile cues are calibrated to support a calm, exhibit-style browsing rhythm.
  • My Collection reuses the same gallery and detail presentation language while keeping user-owned images separate from the curated release dataset.
Privacy & Collection Data

My Collection Notes

  • User-imported banknote images and collection notes are stored locally in app-managed storage.
  • The current version does not provide account-based collection sync across devices.
  • Reference lookup uses text queries only; imported banknote images are not uploaded for remote image analysis.
  • NumisGallery does not provide built-in export, share, or print workflows for banknote imagery.

System Requirements

- iOS 18.0 or later

- iPhone portrait workflow optimized for immersive study presentation
