Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026
BaudHub is a Modbus RTU/TCP and serial debugger for Mac.

This policy explains how BaudHub handles data. In short: we don't collect it. BaudHub talks only to the devices you connect, and your sessions stay on your Mac.

1. No Data Collection

BaudHub does not collect personal data and does not send anything you work with to the developer.

  • No BaudHub account or sign-in
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking, fingerprinting or sale of data
  • No upload of register values, frame logs, write audit logs, sessions or templates

Device addresses, register maps and readings leave your Mac only when you export or share a file yourself.

2. Network Connections

BaudHub opens network connections only for the following:

  • Modbus TCP devices: the host and port you enter for a connection, for example an inverter, charger, meter gateway or PLC.
  • Slave simulator: when you start a Modbus TCP simulator, it listens on the port you choose. Your own Mac, and other computers on your network that can reach that port, can connect to it until you stop the simulator.
  • Apple services: the App Store for purchases and restores, and iCloud key-value storage for the expiry date of a Site Pass (see section 5).
  • Driver links: when an adapter needs a vendor driver, the vendor's download page opens in your browser only if you click the link.

The Modbus TCP protocol has no encryption or authentication. Use it on networks you trust. macOS may ask whether BaudHub may find and connect to devices on your local network; you can change that answer in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

3. Serial Ports and USB Adapters

BaudHub lists the serial ports on your Mac and opens only the port you select, with exclusive access while it is connected. To name adapter chips and suggest drivers, it reads the USB vendor and product identifiers of attached adapters. This information stays on your Mac.

4. Local Storage Only

BaudHub saves your workspace so it can restore it on the next launch. It is written periodically and when you quit, inside the app's sandbox container:

~/Library/Containers/com.foowei.BaudHub/Data/Library/Application Support/BaudHub/

  • Connections (serial port and line settings, TCP hosts and ports), devices and register definitions
  • Last values, value history, frame logs and the write audit log
  • Simulators, imported templates and app preferences
  • Cached purchase and Site Pass status

Extra app instances keep their own copy, which BaudHub deletes at a later launch once that instance has been unused for a day. Session packages, frame log CSV files, terminal recordings and exported templates are saved only where you choose. Copy Diagnostic places text on the clipboard only when you use it.

5. Purchases and Site Passes

BaudHub Pro and the 7-Day and 30-Day Site Passes are sold through the App Store. Apple processes payment; BaudHub uses StoreKit to learn which features are unlocked and never receives your payment details, billing address or Apple Account credentials.

So that a Site Pass bought on one Mac also works on your other Macs, BaudHub stores the pass product and its expiry date in iCloud key-value storage under your Apple Account. Nothing else is stored in iCloud. Purchases are subject to Apple's privacy policy and terms.

6. No Third-Party SDKs

BaudHub is built only with Apple frameworks. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. Files are opened and saved through the standard macOS open and save panels.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide anonymized crash reports to the developer. You control that setting.

7. Your Rights and Data Removal

Because we don't collect data, there is no server-side profile to request, correct or delete. To remove local data, delete connections, devices and simulators in the app, delete exported files you no longer need, or delete BaudHub together with its container folder ~/Library/Containers/com.foowei.BaudHub.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Does BaudHub upload my register values or frame logs?

No. Readings, frames and audit entries stay on your Mac unless you export and share them yourself.

Can other computers see my simulator?

Only while a Modbus TCP simulator is running, and only if they can reach the port it listens on. The simulator shows its listen addresses. A serial (RTU) simulator is reachable only through the port you select.

Does BaudHub see my payment information?

No. Apple handles billing. BaudHub only receives which products are active.

Does BaudHub work offline?

Yes. Serial connections, local network devices and the simulator need no internet connection. Purchases, restores and Site Pass sync between Macs use Apple's services.

9. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when BaudHub's behavior or legal requirements change. Changes are noted by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
