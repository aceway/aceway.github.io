DOMAIN INSIGHT

Clear Metadata & Auto Mosaic

Every digital photo carries hidden metadata known as Exif. It records not just aperture and shutter speed, but often your precise GPS coordinates. ShareGuard leverages the low-level ImageIO framework to surgically strip this invisible data without re-encoding pixels, ensuring your digital footprint remains yours alone. Whether you’re preparing screenshots, photos, or documents, ShareGuard provides flexible masking options and detailed export controls. Choose from color masks, mosaic-style masks, or smart detection modes that automatically recognize sensitive content.
