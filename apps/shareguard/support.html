ShareGuard

Safe sharing for photos, screenshots, and documents with private on-device protection.

Key Features

  • Safe Share Presets: Apply privacy protection faster with clear export choices.
  • Photo, Screenshot, and Document Protection: Review sensitive content before sharing.
  • Automatic Detection: Detect faces, text, QR codes, barcodes, and other sensitive content.
  • Metadata Cleanup: Remove hidden metadata such as device, camera, location, and time details.
  • Batch Processing: Handle larger groups of images more efficiently.
  • Precise Editing: Fine-tune masks with simple, interactive controls.
  • Local Processing: Everything stays on your device.

Technical Specifications

Requires iOS 16, macOS 12 or later.
Works fully offline with no account, upload, or network dependency.
Built for private, local image handling across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
